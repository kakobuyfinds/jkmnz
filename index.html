Sizing and warehouse QC checklist

  1. Save the exact variant. Record the seller URL, item number, size and color so you can compare them at the warehouse.
  2. Compare like-for-like measurements. Measure a garment you own laid flat. Check whether the size chart shows flat chest width or full circumference. For shoes, compare insole length in centimeters.
  3. Review photos before parcel dispatch. Ask for a ruler photo if the measurements are unclear. Check both shoes or both sides of a garment and confirm the agent’s return deadline before approving shipment.

Estimate the full order cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery to the warehouse, agent or payment fees, and your share of international shipping. Include any destination taxes or handling fees that apply.

Example for one item
¥145 item + ¥10 domestic delivery + ¥8 service and payment fees + ¥85 international shipping = ¥248 before any destination charges. The fee and shipping figures are examples. Recalculate using the packed parcel’s weight and dimensions and a current quote for your destination.

Frequently asked questions

What do I do if a product link stops working?

Search HipobuyFind using the catalog item number. If the original seller listing is unavailable, confirm that any replacement offers the same size, color and measurements.

What are warehouse QC photos?

They are photos of the item received by the warehouse. Use them to check the selected variant, visible condition and requested measurements before international shipping.

Why did my shipping quote change?

Carriers may price parcels using actual or volumetric weight. A box that is large for its weight can cost more on a route that bills by volume. Compare the final quotes using the same destination and packed dimensions.
